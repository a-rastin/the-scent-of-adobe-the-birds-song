در سفر

هرکس به مقصد می‌رسد

می‌ایستد...

من سفر را دوست دارم

مقصد من...

رفتن است...
\
\
\
**تهران ۱۳۷۷**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
On a journey,

whoever reaches the destination

stops...

I love the journey;

my destination...

is going...
\
\
\
**Tehran, 1998–99**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Raftan` (to go) can also mean "to pass away." At his age (about 59 by then) the last line may carry a quiet second sense of death as just another departure, though this is only one possible reading.

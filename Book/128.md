**آزادی پشتِ درهای بسته...**
\
\
\
آزادی پشتِ دری بسته...

از روزنهٔ یک بوسه...

مرا به باغی برد... که بارانش،

گل‌های یاس بودند.

در سایهٔ درختانِ بیدمجنون،

کودکی‌ام را دیدم...

که با شصت سالگی‌ام...

آب‌بازی می‌کرد...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۴/۱۱/۲۴**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**Freedom Behind Closed Doors...**
\
\
\
Freedom, behind a closed door...

through the small opening of a kiss...

took me to a garden... whose rain

was jasmine blossoms.

In the shade of the weeping willows,

I saw my childhood...

playing in the water...

with my sixty-year-old self...
\
\
\
**13 February 1996**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bid-majnun` is the weeping willow. Its name means "the willow of Majnun," the love-mad hero of the Persian romance *Layli and Majnun*.
- `Āb-bāzi` means playing or splashing in water, a child's game.

گُلِ خوبم...

دل و جانم تو باشی...

کویرم... ابر و بارانم تو باشی...

بهارِ بی‌زمستانم تو باشی...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My lovely flower...

may you be my heart and soul...

I'm a desert... may you be my cloud and rain...

may you be my spring without winter...
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `To bāshi`, "may you be," closes three lines in a row, so the poem reads like a short song or a lullaby.

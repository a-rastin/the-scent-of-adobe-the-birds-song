شب را

چگونه صبح خواهم کرد...

وقتی تو رفته‌ای...

مهتاب مانده است

و... مرغ شب ترانهٔ تاریکی را

یک ریز خوانده است...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۳/۶/۲۲**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
How will I

make it through the night till morning...

now that you have gone...

the moonlight has stayed,

and... the night bird has sung

the song of darkness on and on, without a pause...
\
\
\
**13 September 1994**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Shab rā sobh kardan` means literally "to turn the night into morning." It's the idiom for staying up through a sleepless night until dawn.
- `Morgh-e shab` means "the night bird," often the nightingale or an owl. `Yek-riz` means nonstop, on and on.

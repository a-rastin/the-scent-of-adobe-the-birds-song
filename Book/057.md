**شب یلدا... هجرانی...**
\
\
\
چه شبی بود آن شب...

رفته بودی تو... و...

بوی خوشِ تو...

در دلم دانهٔ حسرت می‌کاشت...

یادِ دستانِ تو بر پیکر من

سنگ از چشمهٔ اشکم بر می‌داشت...

چه شبی بود آن شب...

رفتی و یادِ تو ماند...

کاش می‌دانستی...

تا سحر با یادت...

وصل می‌زد... هجران می‌خواند...
\
\
\
**زمستان ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**Yalda Night... a Night of Parting...**
\
\
\
What a night that was...

you had gone... and...

your sweet scent...

was planting a seed of longing in my heart...

the memory of your hands on my body

was lifting the stone from the spring of my tears...

What a night that was...

you left, and your memory stayed...

If only you knew...

until dawn, with your memory,

union played... and parting sang...
\
\
\
**Winter 1989–90**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Hejrāni` ("of parting") echoes poem 44's `navā-ye hejrāni`. The last line plays with music: `vasl mizad` ("union was playing," as on an instrument) and `hejrān mikhānd` ("parting was singing"). It's like a duet of union and separation that lasts all night.
- `Dāne` ("seed") comes back from the Sara poem.

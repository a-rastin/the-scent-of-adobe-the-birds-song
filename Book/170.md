دل من انار هزاردانه‌ای‌ست

با توانِ سرخ عشقی

در هر دانه...

وقتی تو به من نگاه می‌کنی

یکی از دانه‌ها سرخ‌تر می‌شود...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My heart is a thousand-seeded pomegranate,

with the red strength of a love

in every seed...

whenever you look at me,

one of the seeds turns redder...
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Anār-e hezār-dāne`, the "thousand-seeded pomegranate," is a well-loved Persian image. The pomegranate is the fruit of Yalda night and stands for love and abundance.
- `Tavān` means power or strength, so each seed holds the "red power" of a love.

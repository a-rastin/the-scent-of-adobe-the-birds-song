هر دو خوابیدیم

خواب را دیدیم

که لَبِ چشمهٔ جوشان خیال

با نخِ خاطره‌ها

شعر می‌بافد...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
We both fell asleep,

we dreamed the dream

that by the bubbling spring of imagination

with the thread of memories

it weaves poetry...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Lab-e cheshmeh` is literally "the lip of the spring," meaning its edge.
- `Khiyāl` (imagination) appears again, as in 180 and 189.

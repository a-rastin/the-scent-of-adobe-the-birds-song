در من ابری نیست...

خندهٔ خورشیدی‌ات را رها کُن...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
There's no cloud in me...

let your sunny laughter go free...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Khandeh-ye khorshidi` is "sun-like laughter," and `rahā kon` means "let it loose" or "set it free."

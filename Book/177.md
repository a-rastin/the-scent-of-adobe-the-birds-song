ما در تمامِ طولِ شب

بی راهه رفتیم...

یک منزلِ یک روزه را

یک ماهه رفتیم...

وقتی رسیدیم...

چشمانِ شب را بسته بودند...

تاریک بود...

راه سفر را بسته بودند
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
All night long

we went the wrong way...

a stage of one day's journey

took us a month...

When we arrived...

they had blindfolded the night's eyes...

it was dark...

they had closed the road of the journey.
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bi-rāheh` means "off the road," "astray," or the wrong way. `Manzel` is a caravan stage, one day's march.
- `Bastan` (to close or tie) appears twice. First they "closed the night's eyes," meaning blindfolded it and left no moon or stars. Then they "closed the road." The unnamed "they" suggests something outside them blocking the way, which may be political. That's my reading.

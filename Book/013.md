تک درختی کهن

به سینهٔ دشت...

خسته از باد و حملهٔ طوفان.

دستمالی سپید بر پنجه...

خواهد از لشگرانِ باد اَمان...
\
\
\
**۱۳۴۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
A lone, ancient tree

on the breast of the plain...

weary of the wind and the storm's assault.

A white handkerchief in its hand...

it begs the armies of the wind for mercy...
\
\
\
**1961**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Amān khāstan` means to plead for quarter or safe passage, the way a defeated side asks an army to spare it. That's why the wind becomes "armies."

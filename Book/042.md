ای... آنِ پرطراوتِ بارانی

مهتابِ بی‌دریغِ بیابانی

چون از سفر به چشمِ تو برگردم

با شعله‌های آتشِ پنهانی

هر برگ در نگاه تَرَم باغی‌ست

هر غنچه شهرِ شادِ چراغانی.
\
\
\
**بهار ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
O... you, fresh as a moment of rain,

unstinting moonlight of the desert,

when I come back from my journey to your eyes

with the flames of a hidden fire,

every leaf, in my wet gaze, is a garden,

every bud a joyful city strung with lights.
\
\
\
**Spring 1368 (spring 1989)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bi-darigh` means "generous, unstinting," the way desert moonlight pours down without holding anything back.
- `Negāh-e taram` ("my wet gaze") means eyes wet with tears of joy, so everything looks larger and more alive.
- A `cherāghāni` is a festive illumination, when a city is strung with lights for a celebration.

عمداً... به اتفاق...

از زیرِ ابر گذر کردیم...

بارانِ اتفاق از ابرِ عمد بارید...

دلی که خاکی‌تر بود... خیس‌تر شد...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۳/۴/۵**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
On purpose... by chance...

we passed beneath the cloud...

the rain of chance fell from the cloud of purpose...

the heart that was earthier... got wetter...
\
\
\
**26 June 1994**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Khāki` means earthy, and also humble or down-to-earth. The humbler heart soaked up more of the rain, so it was moved more deeply.
- `Khis` means soaked through, a more vivid word than plain `tar` (wet).

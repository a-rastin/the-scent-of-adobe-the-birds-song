**به دخترم سایه**
\
\
\
بوی یاسِ سپید را

شمعدانی‌هایم شنیدند...

سرخ‌تر شدند...

یاسِ سپیدِ من... کم‌رنگم...
\
\
\
**تیر ۱۳۷۱**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**To my daughter Sayeh**
\
\
\
My geraniums

heard the scent of the white jasmine...

they turned redder...

my white jasmine... my pale one...
\
\
\
**late June to late July 1992**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Buy-e yās rā shenidand` means literally "they heard the scent." Persian idiom does use `shenidan` (to hear) for smells, but here it also ties into the poet's habit of mixing up the senses.
- `Kam-rang` means pale or light-colored. Tacked on as `kam-rangam`, "my pale one," it reads as a gentle, almost whispered term of endearment.

دو حبّه انگور... به یادِ هم افتادند...

خورشید آمد... شیرین شدند...

باران بارید... پر آب شدند...

مهتاب آمد... بی تاب شدند...

جوشیدند و... شراب شدند...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Two grapes... thought of each other...

The sun came... they grew sweet...

The rain fell... they grew full of juice...

The moonlight came... they grew restless...

They fermented, and... became wine...
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bi-tāb` means "restless" or "unable to bear it." It's his `tāb/tab` wordplay again, because `tāb` is also "glow." The moonlight's glow takes away their composure.
- `Jushidan` is to boil or ferment, and figuratively to seethe with passion.

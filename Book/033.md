تنها یک چیز را از تو

بیشتر دوست دارم،

این که... تو را دوست دارم...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۷**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
There's only one thing

I love more than you:

that... I love you...
\
\
\
**1988**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Tanhā yek chiz rā az to bishtar dust dāram` can mean "I love only one thing more than you" or "there's only one thing about you I love more." Either way the turn comes at the end: what he loves most is the loving itself.

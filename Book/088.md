**دههٔ شصت بر من چگونه گذشت؟**
\
\
\
دههٔ شصت را...

در مهتاب شمردم...

دوازده بهارِ کوتاه بود...

در یک زمستانِ بلند...

در آفتاب شمردم...

سیزده زمستانِ کوتاه بود...

در یک بهارِ بلند...
\
\
\
اگر ریشه در خاک داری می‌رویی...

چه باور کنی چه انکار...

برگ‌ها و شاخه‌ها را سرما و گرما می‌سوزاند...

اما ریشه‌ها را نه...
\
\
\
می‌پرسی دههٔ شصت چگونه گذشت؟

این ضرب‌المثل را هیچ کجا نشنیده‌ام...

«تفنگت را که گرفتند...

تیر و کمان کودکی‌ات...

خطا نمی‌کند...»
\
\
\
**بهمن ۱۳۶۹ در پاسخ به مجلهٔ گردون**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**How Did the Sixties Pass for Me?**
\
\
\
The sixties...

I counted them by moonlight...

they were twelve short springs...

inside one long winter...

I counted them by sunlight...

they were thirteen short winters...

inside one long spring...
\
\
\
If you have roots in the soil, you grow...

whether you believe it or deny it...

cold and heat scorch the leaves and branches...

but not the roots...
\
\
\
You ask how the sixties passed?

Here is a proverb I've never heard anywhere...

"When they've taken your gun...

the bow and arrow of your childhood...

won't miss..."
\
\
\
**January–February 1991, in reply to *Gardoon* magazine**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- "The sixties" (`dahe-ye shast`) here means the 1360s of the Solar Hijri calendar, roughly 1981 to 1991. That decade covered the post-revolution years, the eight-year war and severe hardship. It had just ended when he wrote this, and the poem is his answer to a magazine's question about it.
- `Gardoon` (`Gardun`) was a literary and arts magazine of that period.

در کوچه پر برف تو

به انتظار ایستاده‌ام

هوا آفتابی است

تو را می‌بینم که خندان میایی

من داغ می‌شوم...

سایه‌ام در برف فرو می‌رود
\
\
\
**زمستان ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
In your snow-filled lane

I stand waiting.

The weather is sunny.

I see you coming, laughing.

I grow hot...

my shadow sinks into the snow.
\
\
\
**Winter 1989**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Dāgh mishavam` means "I grow hot" or "I'm burning." His heat makes him sink into the snow.
- `Havā` means both "air" and "longing," so "the weather is sunny" also hints that his longing is bright.

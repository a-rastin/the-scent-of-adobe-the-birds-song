دوتا کفتر سفید و زعفرونی

نشستن دِ نثارِ سایه بونی

سفیده ناله سو... سیلِ شفق کِرد

سه‌کو... اَفتو مِرَه مِثه جِوونی
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۴۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Two pigeons, one white, one saffron,

perched at the edge of the awning's shade.

Dawn broke weeping... and looked upon the twilight;

look... the sun is setting, like youth...
\
\
\
**The 1960s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- This is in a regional dialect, not standard Persian. It is Borujerdi, which fits his ties to Borujerd.

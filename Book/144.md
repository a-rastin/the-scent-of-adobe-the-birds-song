گوشِ من زمزمه‌ای می‌شنود...

تو لبانت خاموش...

تو نگاهت هوسِ باران را...

روی برگِ تشنه می‌بیند...

تو... نگاهت در من آتش افروخت...

هَوَسِ بوسه... لبانم را سوخت...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My ear hears a murmur...

your lips silent...

your gaze sees the longing for rain...

on the thirsty leaf...

you... your gaze lit a fire in me...

the longing for a kiss... burned my lips...
\
\
\
**1999–2000**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Havas` (longing, craving) comes twice. First it's the thirsty leaf's longing for rain, then his own longing for a kiss. Water and fire face each other: the thirst for rain turns into a burning.
- `Khāmush` (silent) is also used for a fire that has gone out, which makes "lit a fire" a quiet answer to it.

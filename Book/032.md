آه... آه...

آه از چشم‌های نیمه بازِ تو

بعد از بلندترین...

بوسه‌هایِ خاکستری باران...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۷**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Ah... ah...

ah, your half-open eyes

after the longest...

gray kisses of the rain...
\
\
\
**1988**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bolandtarin` means both "longest" and "loudest." It's the same word as the "loudest cry" in poem 29, so the line can also be read as "after the loudest...", left hanging by the ellipsis.

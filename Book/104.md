ای ابرِ عابر...

از ماه بگذر...

بی‌مهتاب...

سایه‌ها می‌میرند...

شعر چوپانیم را در دشت خواندم...

شبان... گوسفندانش را رها کرد...

پونه‌ها را بویید...

آنقد گریه کرد... تا... بی‌مهتاب به خواب رفت...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۱/۱/۲۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
O passing cloud...

move on past the moon...

without moonlight...

the shadows die...

I recited my shepherd's poem on the plain...

the shepherd... let his sheep go...

he smelled the wild mint...

and wept so long... that... without moonlight, he fell asleep...
\
\
\
**18 April 1992**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `She'r-e chupāni-am` means "my shepherd's poem," a pastoral or folk-style poem. When the shepherd hears it, he's so moved that he forgets his flock.
- `Puneh` is wild mint (pennyroyal), which grows by streams and has a strong smell of the countryside.
- `Ānqad` is the spoken form of `ānqadr`, "so much." The poem turns colloquial right at its emotional peak.

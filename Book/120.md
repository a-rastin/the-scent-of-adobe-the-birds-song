من از تو سیر نخواهم شد...

کویر از باران...

و دشت از آهو...

و کوه از تیهو...

مُریدِ چشم توام...

کف به دَف زَنَم

هو... هو...
\
\
\
**بهار ۱۳۷۳**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I will never have enough of you...

just as the desert never tires of rain...

nor the plain of the gazelle...

nor the mountain of the partridge...

I am a disciple of your eyes...

I strike my palm upon the daf,

Hu... Hu...
\
\
\
**Spring 1994**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Tihu` is the see-see partridge, a small bird of the mountains. The partridge came up before, in poem 95.
- `Morid` means a disciple in Sufism, someone devoted to a spiritual master. Here the poet is the disciple and the beloved's eyes are the master.

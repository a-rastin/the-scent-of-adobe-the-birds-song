شوقِ یک قطرهٔ باران به چمن،

شور یک بوسهٔ تو بر لبِ من،

مهربانی... ماه...

باران... و بهار...

و همین...

سالِ نو یعنی این...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۶/۱۲/۲۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
The eagerness of a raindrop for the grass,

the thrill of your kiss on my lips,

kindness... the moon...

rain... and spring...

and just this...

this is what the New Year means...
\
\
\
**19 March 1998**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Showq` (eagerness) and `shur` (passion, thrill) open the first two lines with near-matching sounds, and `chaman` (grass) rhymes with `lab-e man` (my lips).

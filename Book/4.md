

پرستوها به طاقِ آسمونند

افق‌ها سایه روشن گرمِ خونند

نمی‌دونی که مرغانِ خیالت

به طاقِ سینه‌ام با درد چونند...
\
\
\
**۱۳۳۹**
\
\
\


**English**
\
\
\
The swallows are up in the arch of the sky,

the horizons, in light and shadow, warm with blood.

You don't know how the birds of your memory

fare with their pain in the arch of my chest...
\
\
\
**1960**

**Notes:**
- `Tāq` is a vaulted arch. The poem uses it twice, first for the sky and then for the chest, so the birds that fly in the sky also live inside the speaker.

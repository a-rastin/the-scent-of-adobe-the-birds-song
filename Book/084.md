در نگاهم آرام...

در نَفَسم پرموج...

به تو می‌اندیشم...

در هر حضیض... در هر اوج...
\
\
\
**اصفهان ۱۳۶۹/۱۰/۳**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Calm in my gaze...

full of waves in my breath...

I think of you...

in every low... in every high...
\
\
\
**Isfahan, 24 December 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Hazīz` and `owj` are a fixed Arabic-derived pair meaning the lowest point and the highest point. In astronomy they're the perigee and apogee, and more generally the lows and peaks of life. I translated them as "every low, every high," but "in every ebb and every peak" would also work.
- `Mowj` (wave) and `owj` (peak) rhyme. That makes a small sea image: calm on the surface, waves underneath, rising and falling.

صفای خنده‌هایت باورم داد

نگاهی عاشق و چشمی تَرَم داد

مِه و طوفان و شب در راه من بود

هوای عشقِ تو بال و پَرَم داد.
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۵۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
The warmth of your laughter gave me faith;

it gave me a loving gaze and tearful eyes.

Mist and storm and night lay on my road;

the longing for your love gave me wings and feathers.
\
\
\
**The 1970s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Safā` means purity, warmth and sincerity together, so "the `safā` of your laughter" is laughter that's open and heartfelt.
- `Havā` is the double meaning again: "air," which you need to fly, and "longing." Her love is both the wind under his wings and the desire that gives them to him.

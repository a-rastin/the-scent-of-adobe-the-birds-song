هشت‌سال...

شاگردی کبوتران کردم...

پرواز نیاموختم...

پروازِ بی‌بال...

مثلِ آوازِ بی‌حال...

مثلِ شعر... مثلِ نقاشی...

اگر عاشق نباشی... آموختنی نیست...
\
\
\
**فروردین ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
For eight years...

I was an apprentice to the pigeons...

I did not learn to fly...

flight without wings...

like song without feeling...

like poetry... like painting...

if you are not in love... cannot be learned...
\
\
\
**March–April 1991**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
`Shāgerdi kardan` means serving as an apprentice to a master, here the pigeons.
- The "eight years" may point to the eight years of the Iran–Iraq War (1980–88), but the poem doesn't say so, so that's only one reading.
- `Āvāz-e bi-hāl` is singing that has no `hāl`, the inner mood or ecstasy that Persian music depends on. He says flight, song, poetry and painting all come only from love, never from technique.
- `Bāl` (wing) and `hāl` (inner state) rhyme, which links the image of flight to the idea of feeling.

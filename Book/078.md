بلندترین نَفَسم، ابری...

کوتاه‌ترین نگاهت، آتشی...

به انتظارِ رعدی باش...

گوش‌هایم بی‌تابِ صدای تو...

اگر تو نخوانی...

وای من... وای تو...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۳/۲۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My longest breath, a cloud...

your briefest glance, a fire...

wait for a thunderclap...

my ears are restless for your voice...

if you don't sing...

woe is me... woe is you...
\
\
\
**19 June 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Khāndan` can mean to sing or to read and recite, so "if you don't sing" can also be heard as "if you don't call to me" or "if you don't read me."
- `Vāy-e man... vāy-e to` is a common Persian cry of grief or warning, like "woe to me... woe to you." It suggests that both of them would lose something.

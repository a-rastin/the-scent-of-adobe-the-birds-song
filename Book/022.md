در عبور شب

گِرد گیسویِ تو می‌گردم

مست از بوی تو می‌گردم

لب به گوشِ تو می‌کنم نجوا

این منم پنجاه

داغ‌تر از تب

سردتر از آه

خنده‌ات با اشک من همراه...

چند مهتاب دگر؟

چند قرصِ ماه؟
\
\
\
**تهران، دی‌ماه ۱۳۵۱**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
As the night passes,

I circle around your hair,

I wander, drunk on your scent,

I put my lips to your ear and whisper:

here I am, fifty,

hotter than fever,

colder than a sigh,

your laughter keeping company with my tears...

How many more moonlit nights?

How many more full moons?
\
\
\
**Tehran, Dey 1351 (December 1972 to January 1973)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Gardidan` is used twice here. "Circle around your hair" carries the old Persian sense of circling someone out of devotion, almost like a sacrifice. "I wander, drunk" is the second meaning.
- `In manam panjāh`, "here I am, fifty," most likely gives the speaker's age. That's why the poem ends by asking how many moonlit nights and full moons are left.
- `Qors-e māh`, "the disc of the moon," means the full moon.

**با یاد دوستم ایرج زند**
\
\
\
تا به گلستان نرسی&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;روح رها نمی‌شود

این همه شفافی تو&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;قسمت ما نمی‌شود

اشک فرشته‌های تو&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;قاصدکِ رهایی‌اند

تا به مژه نَنشسته‌اند&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;شور به پا نمی‌شود

کاش که شور می‌شدم&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;شوق و سرور می‌شدم

مثل تو و فرشته‌ات&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;یک‌شبه نور می‌شدم

گرچه که بندهای ما&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;یک‌شبه وا نمی‌شود

وین رَهِ پرنشیب را&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;دس به عصا نمی‌شود

این همه آزادی تو&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;قسمتِ ما نمی‌شود

تا به گلستان نَرَسی&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;روح رها نمی‌شود
\
\
\
**زمستان ۱۳۸۵**

**آخرین نمایشگاه ایرج زند در گالری گلستان/ فرشته‌های شفاف**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**In memory of my friend Iraj Zand**
\
\
\
Until you reach the rose garden, the soul is not set free;

all your transparency will never be our share.

Your angels' tears are dandelion-messengers of freedom;

until they settle on the eyelash, no passion stirs.

I wish I'd become passion, become longing and joy,

like you and your angel, I'd become light overnight.

Though our bonds don't come undone overnight,

and this steep, downhill road can't be walked cautiously with a cane,

all your freedom will never be our share;

until you reach the rose garden, the soul is not set free.
\
\
\
**Winter 2006–07**

**Iraj Zand's last exhibition at Golestan Gallery / "Transparent Angels"**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Golestān` works three ways: the rose garden, paradise, and the gallery's name. "Until you reach Golestan the soul isn't freed" means both reaching heaven and arriving at that final show.
- `Qāsedak` (dandelion seed head) means literally "little messenger." In Persian folklore it brings news.
- `Dast be 'asā` means "cautiously, carefully," literally "hand on a cane"; `das` is the colloquial spelling. The steep road of life, or of dying, can't be taken carefully.

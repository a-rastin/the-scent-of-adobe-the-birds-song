عاشقِ بارانم...

عشق را ابر...

تو را...آسمان می‌دانم...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I'm in love with rain...

Love I take to be the cloud...

and you... the sky...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- The verb `midānam`, "I consider" or "I take to be," appears once at the end and serves all three lines.

آفتابِ لب بامی بودی

یا طنینِ دلِ جامی بودی

از دلت هیچ نگشتم آگه

غزلِ نیمه تمامی بودی...
\
\
\
**۱۳۴۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
You were sunlight at the edge of a roof,

or the echo from the heart of a cup.

I never came to know your heart.

You were a half-finished ghazal...
\
\
\
**1961**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Āftāb-e lab-e bām`, "sun at the edge of the roof," is a Persian idiom for the last light before sunset. It's used for something fleeting, or for someone near the end of their life.
- A `ghazal` is the classic Persian lyric love poem, so a "half-finished ghazal" is a love that was never completed.

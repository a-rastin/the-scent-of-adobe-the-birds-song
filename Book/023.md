**به دخترم سایه**
\
\
\
لحظه‌ها

مثلِ دانه‌های کوچکند

روی خاکِ خوب

سبز می‌شوند

رشد می‌کنند

میوه می‌دهند.

خاکِ خوب

خاکِ عاشق است

خاکِ تر،

خاکِ پُرثمر...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۶۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**To my daughter, Sayeh**
\
\
\
Moments

are like little seeds.

On good soil

they sprout,

they grow,

they bear fruit.

Good soil

is loving soil,

moist soil,

fruitful soil...
\
\
\
**The 1980s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Sāyeh`, the daughter's name, means "shadow" or "shade," which ties her quietly to the shadows that run through the earlier poems.
- `Khāk-e āsheq` literally means "lover soil." It's soil that's in love, and so ready to make things grow.
- `Tar` means moist or fresh, and figuratively tender.

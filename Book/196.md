همیشه آواز برای شنیدن نیست...

گاه خواندن...

همچون نمک بر زخم...

برای بیدار ماندن است...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
A song isn't always for listening...

sometimes singing...

like salt on a wound...

is for staying awake...
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Namak bar zakhm`, "salt on a wound," usually means making pain worse. Here the sting is useful: it keeps the singer from going numb or falling asleep.

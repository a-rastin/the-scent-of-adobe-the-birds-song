نقاشی‌هایم را می‌سرایم...

نه بوم می‌خواهد،

نَه... دیوار...

نَه قصهٔ خریدار

نَه غصه، کُنجِ انبار...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I compose my paintings as poems...

they need no canvas,

no... wall...

no tale of a buyer,

no sorrow of a storeroom corner...
\
\
\
**The 1990s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Misorāyam` means "I compose (verse)," so he writes his paintings as poems instead of painting them. That ties back to the painting imagery in poems 40 and 45.

نَه...

تو را به من راه نمی‌دهند...

یک روزِ آفتابی

پشتِ دیوار زندانم بیا...

سایهٔ تو...

دیوار را مهربان‌تر می‌کند.
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۸/۹/۷**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
No...

they won't let you in to see me...

One sunny day

come stand behind my prison wall...

your shadow...

will make the wall kinder.
\
\
\
**28 November 1989**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Rāh nemidahand` means "they don't let you through," the way a visitor is turned away at a prison gate.

احساسِ عاشقی...

در جانِ باورم... آنقدر تازه است...

گویی که پیش از این...

هرگز نگفته‌ام... من عاشقِ توام...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
The feeling of being in love...

in the soul of my belief... is so fresh...

as if before this...

I had never said... I'm in love with you...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Jān-e bāvaram` literally means "the soul of my belief," the core of what he trusts and feels to be true.

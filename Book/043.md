می‌خواهی بمان،

می‌خواهی برو...

رفتنِ تو،

دل‌انگیزتر از...

آمدن دیگری‌ست...
\
\
\
**تابستان ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Stay if you want,

go if you want...

Your going

is lovelier than...

someone else's coming...
\
\
\
**Summer 1368 (summer 1989)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Mikhāhi bemān, mikhāhi boro` is a casual spoken way of saying "stay or go, it's up to you." It sounds detached at first, and then the last line turns it into a declaration of love.
- `Del-angiz` means "delightful" or "heart-stirring." The ellipsis after "than..." holds back the surprise for a beat.

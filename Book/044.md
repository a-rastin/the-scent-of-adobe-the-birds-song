سَرم به شانهٔ تو با نوای هجرانی

بلندی نَفَسم پُر ز عطرِ حیرانی

دو چشم بسته‌ام و در هوای گریستن

عبور می‌کنم از کوچه‌های بارانی
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۸/۵/۱**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My head on your shoulder, with a song of parting,

the length of my breath full of the scent of bewilderment,

I have closed both eyes, and in the mood for weeping

I pass through rain-soaked streets.
\
\
\
**23 July 1989**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Navā-ye hejrāni` is a "song of separation." `Hejrān` is the classic word for a lover's parting.
- `Dar havā-ye gerestan` is a nice double meaning. `Havā` is "air" or "weather," which fits the rain, and also "longing, a mood for." So he walks in weather that makes him want to cry.

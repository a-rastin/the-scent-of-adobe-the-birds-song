بر ساقهٔ بلندِ تمنا

با شاخه‌های شوق...

پیوند می‌زنم...

وقتی صدای...

پای تو...

از دور می‌رسد... لبخند می‌زنم.
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۵/۲۶**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Onto the tall stem of desire,

with branches of longing...

I graft...

When the sound...

of your footsteps...

arrives from afar... I smile.
\
\
\
**17 August 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Peyvand zadan` is a gardening word for grafting a branch onto a stem. `Peyvand` also means a bond or union, and it's used for marriage. So the grafting is also the joining of two people.
- `Sedā-ye pā`, the sound of footsteps, is a classic Persian image of a loved one arriving. Sohrab Sepehri's collection `Sedā-ye Pā-ye Āb` ("The Sound of Water's Footsteps") is a famous example. I wouldn't call it a deliberate reference here.

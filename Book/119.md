چرا...

عاشق‌تر از عاشقانِ جهان نباشم

من که در غم‌انگیزترین خاکِ زمین

سبز مانده‌ام...

و... در آرزوی خنده‌هایت

در فاصلهٔ دو انفجار... آواز خوانده‌ام...

در سایهٔ موشک‌ها...

بی‌قایق... بی‌اسب...

در آب و بر خاک رانده‌ام...

چرا با چهره و دستی گُشاده...

سرپا نباشم...

چرا عاشق‌تر از عاشقان دنیا نباشم؟...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۳**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Why...

shouldn't I be more in love than all the world's lovers,

I who, in the saddest soil on earth,

have stayed green...

and... longing for your laughter,

have sung in the gap between two explosions...

in the shadow of the missiles...

with no boat... no horse...

I've pressed on through water and over land...

Why shouldn't I stand tall...

with an open face and open hands...

why shouldn't I be more in love than the lovers of the world?...
\
\
\
**1994**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Rāndan` means to drive or press on. He crossed water with no boat and land with no horse, so he got through the hard times with no help.
- `Bā chehre va dasti goshāde` means "with an open face and an open hand," that is, cheerful and generous.

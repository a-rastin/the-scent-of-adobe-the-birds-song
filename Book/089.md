**برای مریم**
\
\
\
همیشه باران

برای روییدن نیست...

گاه پنجه‌های باران...

نسیم را می‌نوازد...

و گاه... همراهِ تند باد...

ویرانه می‌کند...

نگاهِ تو پنجره‌ای‌ست

به خاطرهٔ خواستن...

با هم ساختن... با هم سوختن نیست...

عشق...

در سرزمینِ شوق می‌رویاند...

و در دشتِ هوس خشک می‌کند...

ای ابرِ بی‌قرار...

این بار... ساقهٔ شوقم...

من را دوباره نگاه کن...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۱۲/۲۴**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**For Maryam**
\
\
\
Rain is not always

for growing...

sometimes the fingers of the rain...

play upon the breeze...

and sometimes... with the gale...

it lays waste...

Your gaze is a window

onto the memory of wanting...

Building together... is not burning together...

Love...

makes things grow in the land of longing...

and withers them in the plain of lust...

O restless cloud...

this time... I am the stem of longing...

look at me again...
\
\
\
**15 March 1991**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Panje` means "fingers" or "claws," so the rain's touch can be gentle or destructive. `Navākhtan` means both to caress and to play an instrument, so the rain "plays" the breeze like strings.
- `Bā ham sākhtan, bā ham sukhtan` reverses a common Persian saying. `Sākhtan o sukhtan`, to put up with things and burn together, is the folk ideal of a shared hardship in marriage. The poet says building together is not the same as burning together.
- `Showq` (longing, passion) and `havas` (lust, whim) are set against each other as the fertile land and the dry plain.

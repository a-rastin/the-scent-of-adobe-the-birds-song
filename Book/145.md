عاشق ساز آواز بودم

پدرم گفت حرام است.

شصت سال

آوازهای نخوانده‌ام را

خواب و بیدار

خط خطی کردم

بی آن که

خطی

به دیوار جهان

کشیده باشم...
\
\
\
**تهران ۱۳۷۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I loved music and song;

my father said it was forbidden.

For sixty years,

asleep and awake,

I scribbled

the songs I never sang,

without ever

drawing a line

on the wall of the world...
\
\
\
**Tehran, 2000–01**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Khat-khati kardan` means to scribble or doodle. It suggests that his painting and handwriting became where the unsung songs went.

**به نقاشانِ جوان: قربانی، صادق‌زاده، نو بهار**
\
\
\
جانا... دوباره نگاه کن

نگاه کردن دیدن نیست...

از ابر تا باران...

یک قلم فاصله است...

یک نفس آبی‌تر...

مهتاب خاموش می‌شود.

عمری گذشت... تا

به چشمِ خود دیدم...

چه مشکل است

که با چشم خود ببینم.

از هر مژه به ساحلِ دیدن راهی‌ست...

چه مرزِ حساسی...

باریک و تاریک...

با کفش‌های شوق،

از «مسیر نبض عناصر»\*

عبورکن...

در معبری چنین...

باید چراغ رنگ به دست بگیریم

و در تاریک‌ترین لحظه

با نگاهی تَر

و ذکر «دوستت دارم»

به دنبال پیری بگردیم...

و از او چیزی بپرسیم...

هر کدام مطلب خود را...

در چنین حالی

من از او می‌پرسم...

اِی پیر...

من سال‌هاست در مسیرِ خاکستری‌ام...

کِی می‌توانم یک قطره بیشتر

با سرخ نقاشی کنم...؟

جانا... تو چه می‌پرسی؟...
\
\
\
**بهار ۱۳۶۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**To the young painters: Ghorbani, Sadeghzadeh, Nobahar**
\
\
\
Dear one... look again,

looking is not seeing...

from cloud to rain...

is one brushstroke's distance...

one breath bluer...

the moonlight goes out.

A lifetime passed... until

I saw with my own eyes...

how hard it is

to see with my own eyes.

From every eyelash there is a path to the shore of seeing...

what a delicate border...

narrow and dark...

In the shoes of longing,

cross through

"the path of the pulse of the elements"\*...

In such a passage...

we must hold the lamp of color in our hands,

and in the darkest moment,

with a moist gaze

and the chant of "I love you,"

go looking for an old sage...

and ask him something...

each of us our own question...

In such a state

I ask him...

O sage...

for years I have been on the gray path...

when can I paint

one drop more with red...?

Dear one... what will you ask?...
\
\
\
**Spring 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- The dedication is to three young painters, who are named only by surname: Ghorbani, Sadeghzadeh and Nobahar. The poet is a painter himself, and here he speaks as a teacher or an older colleague. "Nobahar" is set as two words, «نو بهار», which is probably just the typesetting of the name.
- `Qalam` means both a pen and a paintbrush, so "one brushstroke" from cloud to rain is a painter's measure of distance.
- `Jānā` ("dear one, my soul") is a classical form of address from ghazal poetry.
- `Pir` is the Sufi elder or guide that seekers go to with their questions. `Zekr` is the Sufi practice of repeating a sacred phrase, and here the phrase is "I love you."

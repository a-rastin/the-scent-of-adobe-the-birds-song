با خیالِ تو

گیسوانم سپیدی را...

مو به مو...انکار می‌کند...

شعر کلامِ بیکاره‌ای ست...

که با خیالِ تو...کار می‌کند...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
With the thought of you,

my hair denies its whiteness...

strand by strand...

Poetry is an idle word...

that, with the thought of you... gets to work...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Mu be mu` literally means "hair by hair," and the idiom means "in every detail." Since the subject is his greying hair, the phrase works both literally and as the idiom.
- The last two lines play `bikāreh` ("idle," literally "without work") against `kār mikonad` ("works"). Poetry on its own does nothing, but the thought of her sets it working.

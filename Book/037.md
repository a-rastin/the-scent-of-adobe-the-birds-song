تو به سفری کوتاه می‌روی

من از کوچهٔ بی تو...

من از خانهٔ بی تو... می‌گذرم...

من بی تو می‌گذرم...

برف می‌بارد...

برف بی تو می‌بارد...

برف بر گونه‌هایم در اشک می‌بارد...

من در برف بی تو می‌گذرم...

کوچه خاموش است...

خانه خاموش است...

من بی‌حرف در برف می‌گذرم...
\
\
\
**زمستان ۱۳۶۷**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
You go away on a short trip.

I pass through the street without you...

I pass through the house without you...

I go on without you...

Snow is falling...

snow is falling without you...

snow falls on my cheeks, into tears...

I pass through the snow without you...

The street is silent...

the house is silent...

wordless, I pass through the snow...
\
\
\
**Winter 1367 (winter 1988–89)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Migozaram` means both "I pass through" and "I get by, I spend my time." So `man bi to migozaram` is both "I walk on without you" and "I get through the days without you." I used both senses in the translation.
- `Barf bar gunehāyam dar ashk mibārad` can mean the snow falls onto his tears, or melts into them. Either way the snow and the tears blend together.
- `Khāmush` means both "silent" and "dark, with the lights out," as in a house with nobody home. `Bi-harf` means "without a word."

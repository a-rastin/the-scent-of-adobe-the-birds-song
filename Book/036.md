شعرم را برای تو گفتم...

خندیدی...

با دیگری خواندم...

گریست...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۷/۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I recited my poem to you...

you laughed...

I read it with someone else...

she wept...
\
\
\
**Azar 1367 (November–December 1988)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Bā digari khāndam` literally means "I read it with another," not "to another." That suggests reading it together and sharing it, which makes it feel more intimate. Persian has no gendered pronouns, so `gerist` is just "wept." I used "she," but it could be "he" or "they."

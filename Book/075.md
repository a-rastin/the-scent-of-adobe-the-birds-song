نوروز را از شب آغاز کرده‌ایم...

خورشید در راه است... و...

دست‌هایم پر از دانه‌های نیلوفر...

فردا...

بلندترین سایهٔ تو را گل خواهم کاشت...

و... با چشمی آفتابی...

در انتظار خواهم ماند...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۱/۱**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
We have begun Nowruz from the night...

the sun is on its way... and...

my hands are full of morning-glory seeds...

Tomorrow...

I will plant your tallest shadow with flowers...

and... with a sunny eye...

I will wait...
\
\
\
**21 March 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Niloufar` can mean water lily or lotus, and in Persian poetry it also often means morning glory, a climbing flower. Seeds in the hands point to morning glory, which would climb your "tallest shadow" and fill it with flowers.
- `Bolandtarin sāye-ye to ...gol khāham kāsht` means "I will plant your tallest shadow with flowers." The `rā` sits a little oddly, so it could also be read as "I will plant flowers along your tallest shadow." Shadows are longest at dawn, which brings back the sun that is "on its way."

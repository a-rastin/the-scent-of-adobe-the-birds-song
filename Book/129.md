می‌شود تو را...

مثل بَربَطی نواخت... مثلِ تیهوئی شنید...

مثل آهویی شناخت...

در کدام آسمان پرواز...

در کدامین رود شنا می‌کنی...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۵**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
One could play you...

like a barbat... hear you like a partridge...

know you like a gazelle...

In which sky do you fly...

in which river do you swim...
\
\
\
**1996**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- The `barbat` is the old Persian short-necked lute, the ancestor of the oud. Playing the beloved like an instrument is an image of closeness and harmony.
- The `tihu` (see-see partridge) and the `āhu` (gazelle) come back from poem 120. The partridge's call is something to listen for, and the gazelle is shy and has to be known and approached gently.

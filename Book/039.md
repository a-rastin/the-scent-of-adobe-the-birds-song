**Notes:**
- `Dar fāsele-ye...` ("in the gap between...") brings back the gaps between two explosions from poem 34. Now, though, his soul takes shape in the gaps between loving breaths instead of between bombs.
- `Hāy...` is the same calling-out word he uses in earlier poems.

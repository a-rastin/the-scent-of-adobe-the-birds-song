یک غفلت کوتاه...

یک آه...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
One brief lapse...

one sigh...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Gheflat` means a moment of carelessness or inattention. In Sufi language it also means forgetting what truly matters. A single moment's lapse leaves a lifetime's sigh.
- `Āh` (sigh) is the same word that closes 141 ("a smile... a sigh...").

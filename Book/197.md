هیچ پرنده‌ای

آوازِ دیروز را تکرار نمی‌کند...

تهمتِ تکرار...

در عادَتِ ماست...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
No bird

repeats yesterday's song...

the charge of repetition...

lies in our habit...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Tohmat` means an accusation, often an unfair one. We accuse birds of singing the same song, but the sameness is in our habit of listening and not in their song.
- `Tekrār`, "repetition," appears twice, and ironically so.

در کبودِ چشمِ تو

عهدی‌ست با من...

عهدی از پولاد... عهدی از آهن...
\
\
\
**دههٔ ۱۳۸۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
In the blue of your eyes

there's a pact with me...

a pact of steel... a pact of iron...
\
\
\
**The 2000s**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Kabud` is a deep blue or blue-grey. It's the colour of the sky, but also of steel and of a bruise, which may tie it to "steel" and "iron" in the next line.
- `Ahd` means a vow, pledge, or covenant. Saying it twice, as steel and then as iron, stresses how unbreakable it is.
- `Man` and `āhan` rhyme, which gives the short poem a ring like a vow.

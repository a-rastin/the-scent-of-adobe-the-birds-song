پندار خاکم...

که مرا...

برگ‌های پاییزی پوشانده...

پیش از این که تن به دریا بسپاری،

در من غلطی بزن...

تا عطر پنجاه و پنج بهار،

بر پیکرت بنشیند...

آنک... تو را...

در ململی از خاطره می‌پیچم...

تو را، که...

به دورترین جزیرهٔ جهان سفر خواهی کرد...

انگار خاکم...

ای یار...

صندوقچه‌ای از مرا...

همراه بردار.
\
\
\
**آذر ۱۳۷۳**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Think of me as earth...

earth that

autumn leaves have covered...

Before you give yourself to the sea,

roll about in me...

so the scent of fifty-five springs

may settle on your body...

Here now... you...

I wrap in a muslin of memory...

you, who

will travel to the farthest island in the world...

It's as if I'm earth...

O beloved...

take a small chest of me

along with you.
\
\
\
**December 1994**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Ghalt zadan` means to roll about, the way an animal rolls in earth or grass. He wants his scent to cling to the traveler.
- `Malmal` is muslin, the fine cotton cloth used to wrap precious or delicate things.
- `Sanduqche` is a small keepsake chest. Iranian emigrants often take a handful of their homeland's soil with them, and here the poet offers himself as that soil.

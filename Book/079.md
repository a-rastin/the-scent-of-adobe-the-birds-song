گوش‌هایم در حیرتِ دیدن...

چشم‌هایم در حسرتِ شنیدن...

ای عشق...

من آوازِ تو را نقاشی می‌کنم...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۳/۲۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
My ears, in wonder at seeing...

my eyes, in longing for hearing...

O love...

I paint your song...
\
\
\
**19 June 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Heyrat` (wonder) and `hasrat` (longing) look almost the same in Persian script and differ by one letter. It's the same kind of wordplay as `pish` and `bish` in poem 77.

من به رنگ ابر

و به بوی خاک

و به طعمِ اشک

و به عمق آسمان صمیمی‌ام

تشنه... تشنهٔ

قطره... قطره...

آبی قدیمی‌ام...
\
\
\
**آبان ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I am close to the color of cloud,

the smell of earth,

the taste of tears,

and the depth of the sky.

Thirsty... thirsty

drop... by drop...

for an old blue...
\
\
\
**October–November 1989**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Samimi` means "intimate, sincere, close." The idea is that he's at home with these things: clouds, earth, tears, and sky.

به خاکِ غمَت نشاندی‌ام...

با هزار ریشه... سبز شدم...

برگم بی‌سایه شد،

تا خورشید را از خاک نگیرد...

و... هیچ روییده‌ای...

در خاک نمیرد.
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۲/۶/۲۶**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
You planted me in the soil of your sorrow...

with a thousand roots... I turned green...

my leaves became shadowless,

so they wouldn't take the sun from the soil...

and so... nothing that grows...

would die in the soil.
\
\
\
**17 September 1993**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Neshāndi-am` means "you planted me" or "you set me down." `Neshāndan` is used for planting a tree and also for seating someone.
- `Sabz shodam` means "I turned green" or "I sprouted," the growth that wouldn't come for the caged bird.

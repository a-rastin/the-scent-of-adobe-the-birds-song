چه کوتاه می‌نماید

بلندترین قلهٔ جهان،

درحافظهٔ ابر،

در خاطرهٔ باران...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۰/۱/۲۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
How short it seems,

the highest peak in the world,

in the memory of the cloud,

in the recollection of the rain...
\
\
\
**18 April 1991**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Hāfeze` and `khātere` both mean memory. `Hāfeze` is the ability to remember, and `khātere` is a remembered moment. Using both makes the cloud and the rain seem very old and wise..

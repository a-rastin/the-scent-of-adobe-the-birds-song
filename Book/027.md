تو را دوست دارم

بی باران

و باران را دوست دارم

با تو...

های... با من باش

تا ابر عقیم بر سرزمین گرفتار ببارد

و چوبدست خشک دژخیم

در خاک شب‌زده سبز شود...

با من باش

تا هر تیرک مرگ آور

گیاه برگ آوری شود

و بر اوج مناره‌ها که

کلاغان لانه کرده‌اند

پرستوها زمزمه کنند...

با من باش

تا بجای گریز از بمب‌ها

در پناهگاه‌های بی‌پناه

به چتر درختی ایمن از باران

پناه بریم...

های... تو را دوست دارم...
\
\
\
**تابستان ۱۳۶۶**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I love you

without rain,

and I love the rain

with you...

Oh... be with me,

so the barren cloud may rain on this captive land,

and the executioner's dry staff

may turn green in the night-struck soil...

Be with me,

so every deadly little post

may become a leafing plant,

and on the tops of the minarets

where crows have nested

swallows may murmur...

Be with me,

so that instead of fleeing the bombs

in shelters that give no shelter,

we may take refuge under the umbrella of a tree,

safe from the rain...

Oh... I love you...
\
\
\
**Summer 1987**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Tirak` is a small post or pole. A "deadly post" most likely means a gallows post or an execution stake, which the poem wishes would come back to life as a plant.
- `Aqim`, "barren," appeared in poem 14 too. Here it's the cloud that gives no rain, and the line hopes it will rain anyway.
- `Hāy` is the same cry as in poem 25, but here it's an urgent plea instead of grief. In the Persian calendar the year is 1366, which is 1987.

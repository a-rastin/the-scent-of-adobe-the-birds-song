های... باران...

تو می‌باری...

تا بوی کاهگل را

که فراموش کرده‌ای

به خاطر آوری.

باران ببار...

زنی را که دوست دارم

سفر می‌کند

و چون برگردد

مرا از خاطر برده است...
\
\
\
**تهران، پاییز ۱۳۶۸**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
Hey... rain...

you fall...

so that you remember

the smell of kahgel

you had forgotten.

Rain, fall...

the woman I love

is traveling,

and when she comes back

she will have forgotten me...
\
\
\
**Tehran, autumn 1368 (autumn 1989)**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Barde ast` is in the perfect tense, literally "has forgotten." That makes it sound already settled, as if her forgetting is certain before she even returns. I translated it as "will have forgotten" to keep that sense.

ما نرفتیم...

دوام آوردیم...

تا کبوترها را...

از دلِ چاه... به بام آوردیم...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۵/۱۱/۲۶**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
We didn't leave...

we held on...

until we brought the pigeons...

up from the heart of the well... to the rooftop...
\
\
\
**14 February 1997**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Dovām` (holding on, enduring) rhymes with `bām` (rooftop). Staying put is what lifts the pigeons from the depths into the open sky.

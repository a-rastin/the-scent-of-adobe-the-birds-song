شوقِ ستاره‌ای که...

سُو... سُو می‌زند...

مرا از تاریکی می‌رهاند...
\
\
\
**۱۳۸۲**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
The eagerness of a star that...

flickers... flickers...

frees me from the darkness...
\
\
\
**2003–04**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Su-su zadan` is an everyday expression for a faint light flickering, almost going out. The ellipses between the two `su`s make the line flicker on the page too.

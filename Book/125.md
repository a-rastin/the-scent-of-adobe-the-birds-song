**به حمید مصدق**
\
\
\
خواب... دریای... مه آلودِ... معطر،

و تو با، زورقِ ابر

هر شب از ساحلِ مهر،

در کنارم به سفر می‌آیی...

من و تو در صدفی می‌خوابیم...

عمقِ آبی...

دلِ سرخ...

و نَفَس‌های بنفش...

موج‌هایی می‌برد ما را...

تا دور...

تُو به تُو... دم در دم

پشتِ تاریکی‌ها... تا نور...

نه هراسِ ماندن...

نه شتابِ رفتن...

خواب دریای دل آرامِ صبور.

موج‌ها با هم در کار...

چشم‌ها بسته و دل بیدار...

من و تو در صدفی می‌خوابیم...

ساق در ساقی تر...

مثل دو نیلوفر...

تا سرِ شاخ سحر می‌تابیم...

«خواب رؤیای فراموشی‌هاست»\*

خواب را دریابیم...
\
\
\
**۱۳۷۴**

**\*حمید مصدق**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
**To Hamid Mosaddegh**
\
\
\
Sleep... a misty... fragrant... sea,

and you, in a skiff of cloud,

every night, from the shore of kindness,

come to journey by my side...

You and I sleep inside a seashell...

blue depths...

red hearts...

and violet breaths...

waves carry us...

far away...

layer upon layer... breath into breath,

behind the darkness... toward the light...

no fear of staying...

no rush to leave...

sleep, a sea of a calm and patient heart.

The waves working together...

eyes closed and hearts awake...

You and I sleep inside a seashell...

one moist stem wound around another...

like two morning glories...

we twine our way up to the tip of dawn's branch...

"Sleep is the dream of forgettings"\*

let us seize sleep...
\
\
\
**1995**

**\*Hamid Mosaddegh**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- Hamid Mosaddegh (1939–1998) was a well-known Iranian poet, best remembered for *Ābi, Khākestari, Siyāh* ("Blue, Gray, Black"). The quoted line, which the asterisk credits to him, works as an epigraph and a tribute. Which of his poems it comes from isn't confirmed.
- `Niloufar` can mean water lily or morning glory. Since the two stems twine up to "the tip of dawn's branch," it reads here as the climbing morning glory, which ties back to the morning-glory seeds of poem 75.
- `Mitābim` is ambiguous. It can mean "we twine," as a climbing vine does, or "we shine." The translation uses twining, but both fit the dawn image.

آنقدر ماندم...

آنقدر شعر گفتم...

آنقدر شعر خواندم...

گونه‌هایت تر شد...

گریه‌ات... در باران... باور شد...
\
\
\
**تابستان ۱۳۷۵**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I stayed so long...

wrote so many poems...

read so many poems aloud...

that your cheeks grew wet...

and your tears... in the rain... came to be believed...
\
\
\
**Summer 1996**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `She'r goftan` means to compose a poem, literally "to say" one, and `she'r khāndan` means to recite it. He did both, over and over.

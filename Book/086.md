بر چینه‌های کودکی‌ام

در معبرِ نسیم نشستم

و...

نخ‌های باد را...

بر بسته‌های خاطره...

بستم...
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۱۰/۶**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
On the mud walls of my childhood,

in the breeze's passageway, I sat,

and...

the threads of the wind...

onto the bundles of memory...

I tied...
\
\
\
**27 December 1990**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Chine` is an old wall built of packed mud in layers, the kind that edges orchards and village lanes.
- `Baste` (bundle or parcel) and `bastam` (I tied) come from the same root. It makes a small play on words: tying the wind onto parcels of memory, so the moment is kept.

من

خاموشم

تا ماه... با تو... بگوید.
\
\
\
**فروردین ۱۳۷۰**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
I

am silent

so that the moon... may speak... with you.
\
\
\
**March–April 1991**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- `Khāmush` means both "silent" and "unlit" or "put out." So the speaker holds back his own voice and his own light, and the moon speaks and shines in his place.

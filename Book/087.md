پنجاه سال است

که این قمری

بر شاخهٔ پربرف نشسته است.
\
\
\
**۱۳۶۹/۱۰/۱۹**

-----
-----

**English**
\
\
\
For fifty years

this turtledove

has sat on a snow-laden branch.
\
\
\
**9 January 1991**
\
\
\
**Notes:**
- The `qomri` is the turtledove, a classic bird of Persian poetry, known for its cooing lament and its loyalty.

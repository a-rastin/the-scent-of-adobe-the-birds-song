پرنده‌ای
پشتِ دیوارِ کاهگلی
می‌خواند.
دیوار را نقاشی کردم.
باران بارید
دیوار پاک شد.
بوی کاهگل
با آواز پرنده ماند.

پاییز ۱۳۶۸

English

A bird
behind the mud-and-straw wall
was singing.
I painted the wall.
Rain fell,
the wall was washed clean.
The scent of mud-and-straw
stayed on with the bird's song.

Autumn 1989

Notes: kāhgel is the traditional plaster of mud mixed with straw, and its smell after rain is a very familiar, nostalgic scent in Iran.
